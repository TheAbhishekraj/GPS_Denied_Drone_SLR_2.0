# Cover Letter

**To the Editor-in-Chief**
IEEE Robotics and Automation Letters

**Date:** July 11, 2026

**Subject:** Submission of Manuscript "GPS-Denied Drone Navigation: A Systematic Literature Review of Sensors, Methods, and Localization Accuracy (2015–2025)"

Dear Editor-in-Chief,

We are pleased to submit our manuscript entitled "GPS-Denied Drone Navigation: A Systematic Literature Review of Sensors, Methods, and Localization Accuracy (2015–2025)" for consideration for publication as a Systematic Literature Review in IEEE Robotics and Automation Letters.

This manuscript represents the most comprehensive quantitative synthesis of GPS-denied UAV navigation to date. Following the PRISMA 2020 guidelines, we have systematically reviewed 231 peer-reviewed papers to extract and analyze sensor configurations, navigation algorithms, evaluation environments, and localization accuracy metrics (ATE RMSE).

**Key Highlights of this work include:**
- A detailed quantitative characterization of the sensor and algorithmic landscape across the full 2015–2025 decade.
- Sensor-stratified accuracy benchmarks across 16 distinct configurations and 6 method classes, based on 198 real-flight experiments.
- Identification of five critical emerging directions, including event-camera navigation and deep-learning-based odometry.
- An open, deduplicated database of the 231 papers with standardized metadata to serve as a community resource.

We confirm that this work is original and has not been published elsewhere, nor is it currently under consideration for publication elsewhere. We also confirm that all authors have approved the manuscript and agree with its submission to IEEE Robotics and Automation Letters.

Thank you for your consideration of our work. Please let us know if you require any further information.
